# Applicant Guide
## Unified Landlord Center SF — Rental Application Manual

This guide explains how to apply for a rental unit, what happens after you
submit your application, and how the tenant screening process works.

---

## 1. Submitting a Rental Application

### Where to apply

Go to `{portal-url}/apply` — this page is public and does not require a login.

### What information you will need

Have the following ready before you start. The form takes approximately
10 minutes to complete.

**Personal Information**
- First and last name
- Email address (where you want to be contacted)
- Phone number
- Date of birth

**Current Housing**
- Your current street address
- Monthly rent you currently pay
- Your current landlord's name and phone number (optional but recommended)

**Employment**
- Employer name
- Job title (optional)
- Monthly gross income (before taxes)
- Date you started your current job (optional)

**Rental History**
- Whether you have ever been evicted (Yes / No)
- Whether you have ever broken a lease early (Yes / No)

**Occupants & Pets**
- Total number of people who will live in the unit (including yourself)
- Whether you have pets (Yes / No)
- If yes: breed, size, and number of pets

**Consent** (both required)
- Authorization for a background and credit check
- Certification that all information you provided is accurate

### Submitting

Click **Submit Application** when you are done. You will see a confirmation
message:

> "We received your application and will be in touch within 2 business days."

You do not need to create an account or log in to apply.

---

## 2. After You Apply

Once you submit:

1. **Landlord is notified immediately** — the landlord receives an automatic
   alert with your key details and income information.

2. **Landlord reviews your application** — they will look over the information
   you provided and decide whether to proceed to screening.

3. **You may be contacted** — the landlord may reach out by phone or email
   to schedule a showing or ask follow-up questions before ordering a
   screening report.

---

## 3. Tenant Screening (TransUnion SmartMove)

If the landlord decides to move forward, they will send you a **screening
invitation** via TransUnion SmartMove.

### What you will receive

An email from TransUnion SmartMove with a link to complete your screening.
Check your spam folder if you do not see it within a few minutes of the
landlord telling you it was sent.

### What the screening covers

- **Credit report** — your credit history and score range
- **Criminal background check** — national criminal database search
- **Eviction history** — prior eviction filings

### Who pays for the screening

**You pay for the screening report directly** (not the landlord). The cost
is approximately $40–$75 depending on which report the landlord selects.
Payment is processed securely through TransUnion's website.

### How to complete the screening

1. Open the email from TransUnion SmartMove
2. Click the invitation link
3. Create a TransUnion account or log in to your existing one
4. Verify your identity (TransUnion will ask security questions)
5. Pay for the report with a credit or debit card
6. TransUnion processes the report, usually within minutes

Once complete, the landlord receives the report in their SmartMove dashboard.
You will also have access to your report through your TransUnion account.

### Privacy

Your full credit and criminal report details are visible **only to you and
the landlord who requested the screening**. The landlord cannot see your full
Social Security number or other sensitive identity information.

## 4. Application Status

The landlord tracks applications through the following stages:

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New Application | Application received, pending landlord review |
| Screening Sent | Screening invitation has been sent to you |
| Screened | Your screening report is available for the landlord to review |
| Approved | Your application has been approved |
| Lease Sent | A lease has been sent to you for signature via BoldSign |
| Tenant | You have signed the lease and are now an active tenant |

---

## 5. Lease Signing (if approved)

If your application is approved, the landlord will send you a lease agreement
to sign electronically via **BoldSign**.

1. You will receive an email from BoldSign (noreply@boldsign.com) with a
   **Sign Now** link
2. Review the lease carefully before signing
3. Click each signature and initial field and complete them
4. Click **Finish** to submit your signature

The landlord will countersign. Once both parties have signed:
- A final executed PDF is emailed to you
- Your tenant portal account is set up
- You can log in at `{portal-url}/login` to pay rent and manage your tenancy

## 6. Common Questions

**Can I apply for multiple units?**
Yes, you can submit a new application for each available unit.

**Is my information secure?**
Your application data is stored in the landlord's ERPNext property management
system and is not shared with third parties except for the screening process
(TransUnion SmartMove), which is governed by TransUnion's privacy policy.

**What if I made a mistake on my application?**
Contact the landlord directly by phone or email to provide the correct
information.

**How long does the process take?**
Most landlords review applications within 1–2 business days and send the
screening invitation shortly after. Screening results are typically available
within minutes to a few hours. Total time from application to lease offer is
usually 2–5 business days if everything goes smoothly.

**I haven't heard back after several days.**
Contact the landlord directly. Make sure you provided a valid email address
and phone number on your application.
